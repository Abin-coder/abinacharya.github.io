My research sits at the intersection of natural language processing,
multi-agent systems, and reinforcement learning. I'm especially interested
in how multiple specialized LLM agents can collaborate on complex language
tasks — and how reinforcement learning can be used to optimize that
collaboration.

I'm a PhD student in the
[Language Information and Computation (LINC) Lab](https://labs.uccs.edu/linclab/)
at UCCS, advised by
[Dr. Jugal Kalita](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=dXITneMAAAAJ).
My current work focuses on multi-agent pipelines for long-document
summarization, RL-based fine-tuning of LLM agents, and adaptive intelligent
tutoring systems. I'm also interested in explainable AI and in making
multi-agent systems more flexible and trustworthy.
