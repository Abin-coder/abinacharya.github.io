I'm Abin Acharya, a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of
Colorado, Colorado Springs (UCCS), originally from Nepal. I work in the
Language Information and Computation (LINC) Lab, where my research focuses
on natural language processing, multi-agent systems, and reinforcement
learning.

Before moving to Colorado, I earned my Bachelor of Computer Engineering
from the Institute of Engineering, Tribhuvan University in Kathmandu, and
spent time teaching and working in IT back home.

When I'm not doing research or teaching, you'll probably find me playing
cricket or badminton, taking photographs, listening to music, traveling,
picking up new languages, or reading.
