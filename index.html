Hello! I'm a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Colorado, Colorado Springs, where I work in the Language, Information and Computation (LINC) Lab with {{ site.author.advisor }}. My research sits at the intersection of natural language processing, multi-agent systems, and reinforcement learning in particular, how several specialized LLM agents can collaborate on hard language tasks, and how reinforcement learning can be used to tune that collaboration.

I also serve as a Graduate Teaching Assistant in the CS department where I support 150+ students across Intro to Software Engineering, Advanced Object-Oriented Programming, Python, C# and Graduate Advanced Software Engineering.

I'm originally from Nepal. Before moving to Colorado I earned my Bachelor of Computer Engineering from the Institute of Engineering, Tribhuvan University in Kathmandu, and spent two years teaching and running IT infrastructure at a school in Chitwan.

Outside research you'll find me playing cricket, watching anime, taking photographs, listening to music, traveling, picking up new languages, or reading. Feel free to get in touch.
